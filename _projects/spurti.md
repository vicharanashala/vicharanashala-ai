<p class="product-page-tagline">Engagement tracking that makes effort visible — not just attendance.</p>

<div class="product-page-section">
  <h2>The Problem</h2>
  <p>In a 10-week programme with thousands of students, attendance ticks tell you nothing. Who is actually showing up and participating? Who is quietly slipping through? Instructors have no way to tell, and students have no incentive to do more than the minimum.</p>
</div>

<div class="product-page-section">
  <h2>What We Built</h2>
  <p>Spurti converts daily student actions — attending sessions, answering polls, teaching peers, responding to queries — into a transparent point system. Every six hours, an automated pipeline scores the entire cohort. Students see their points, their rank on live leaderboards, and earn shareable achievement cards they post on LinkedIn. The scoring rubric is fully open: every student can verify exactly how their points were earned.</p>
</div>

  <p>Deployed across the VLED Summership 2026 programme at IIT Ropar. Built on the Samagama stack — same auth, same student records, zero extra sign-ups. React frontend, Express API, MongoDB. Fully open source.</p>
