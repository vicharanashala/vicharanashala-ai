A problem is brought to the community, and people come together to think about it without referring to books, websites, or ready-made solutions. They begin with what they already understand and gradually explore the problem through conversation.

Each person may see the problem differently, suggest a possibility, question an assumption, or build on another person's idea. There is no pressure to arrive at an answer immediately. Instead, understanding develops slowly as the community examines different possibilities and learns from one another.

## Moving forward together

An important part of this process is that the community does not move forward until everyone understands. The person who is taking the longest to understand is not left behind. Instead, the group keeps returning to the idea, explaining it in different ways, questioning it, and finding new ways to make it clear.

Everyone becomes responsible for helping everyone else understand. The goal is not to convince the majority, but to bring the entire group to a shared understanding. Once everyone is able to see the reasoning, the group moves forward together.

<p class="chc-note">Through this process, several possible solutions emerge, are discussed, questioned, compared, and refined. The final solution grows within the community through patience, conversation, reflection, and collective reasoning.</p>
