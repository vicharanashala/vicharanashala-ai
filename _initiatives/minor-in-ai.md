The Minor in Artificial Intelligence (AI) is delivered by IIT Ropar, and four batches of the programme have been completed. The programme is designed to give learners from diverse academic and professional backgrounds both foundational and advanced knowledge in AI. It is currently being offered to university students.

The programme is open to learners from all backgrounds. The only eligibility is completion of 12th grade, and no prior coding experience is required.

## What learners cover

The programme aims to equip participants with the skills and knowledge to apply AI effectively in real-world contexts. It begins with a foundation before moving on to advanced topics.
